Frequently asked questions

What can Smart Fox VTU offer?
The platform is designed for airtime, data, school fees and additional VTU and bill-payment services that can be connected through approved APIs.
Are all services live now?
No. This frontend is being prepared first. Services such as real wallet funding and VTU fulfilment will be connected during the backend and production stages.
Will my money be stored as data or airtime?
No. In the planned architecture, a provider API fulfils airtime/data purchases when a transaction is confirmed.
Can more services be added later?
Yes. The service grid and backend are intentionally being designed to accept additional service integrations over time.
